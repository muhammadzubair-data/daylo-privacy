Daylo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Daylo is a tasks and habits app published by ZubairLabs (Muhammad Zubair). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Daylo works entirely on your device. We do not collect, receive, store or share any of your data.

What the app stores

The tasks, habits, completion history and settings you enter are saved only in your phone's local app storage. This data never leaves your device. We have no servers and no user accounts, and the app has no sign-in.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we never receive it.
  • The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Permissions

Daylo does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Daylo does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
