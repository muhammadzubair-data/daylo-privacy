Hisab Privacy Policy

Effective: 18 November 2026

Hisab: Expense & Budget Tracker is a free app published by ZubairLabs (Muhammad Zubair). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Your records stay on your phone. Hisab does not request internet access, has no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and never sends your records to us.

What the app stores on your phone

Hisab stores your transactions, loan records, budgets, and your language, theme and currency settings in the app's private storage on your Android device. This information is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and it is not included in Android cloud backups.

What the app does not access

Hisab does not access your bank accounts, contacts, location, camera, microphone or your general file storage. No account or sign-in is needed.

Export, backup and restore

CSV export and backup/restore use the Android system file picker. You choose which file to open and where to save backups or CSV files. If you save to a cloud service, that provider's policy applies to the file. Backup and CSV files are ordinary readable files and are not encrypted by Hisab, so please keep them private.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual records, or all local records, in Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage also deletes your local data. Files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them yourself.

Financial information

Hisab is a manual record-keeping tool. It does not connect to banks, move money or give financial advice. Changing the currency only changes the label shown; it does not convert amounts.

Children

Hisab is a general personal record-keeping app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
