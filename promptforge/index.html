PromptForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

PromptForge is a free app published by ZubairLabs (Muhammad Zubair). It helps you turn an idea into a clear prompt that you can copy into an AI tool of your choice. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

PromptForge builds prompts on your device. We do not collect, receive, store or share any of your data. The app does not request internet access, and there are no ads, analytics, tracking or accounts.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The ideas you type or speak, your choices (tool, type, tone, length, language) and the prompts you save to your Library.
  • Your app language setting (English or Urdu).

This information stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in Android cloud backups.

Microphone and voice input

Using the microphone is optional. PromptForge asks for microphone permission only when you tap "Speak your idea". Your speech is turned into text by Android's on-device speech recognizer on your phone. PromptForge does not record, save or send any audio. If on-device voice is not available on your phone, you can type your idea instead. You can turn off microphone permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Copying and sharing

When you tap "Copy prompt", the text is placed on your phone's clipboard so you can paste it into another app. If you share a prompt, Android opens its share menu and sends the text to the app you choose. Any AI tool or other app you paste or share into handles that text under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not send your ideas or prompts to any server, and we do not use cloud AI to generate prompts.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the app does not use an advertising ID.
  • We do not show ads, and there are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we never receive it.

Third-party AI tools

PromptForge mentions AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others only so you can choose which tool your prompt is written for. PromptForge is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by those companies, and it does not connect to their services.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved prompts inside the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

PromptForge is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
