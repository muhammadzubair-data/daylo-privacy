ClipForge AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ClipForge AI – Video Editor is a free video editing app published by ZubairLabs (Muhammad Zubair). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

ClipForge AI works entirely on your device. We do not collect, receive, store or share any of your data, and the app does not request internet access.

What the app handles

  • Videos you choose. When you tap "Import video", Android's file picker lets you pick a video. The app makes a working copy of that video in its own private storage so it can be previewed, trimmed and exported. The app does not browse your gallery and cannot see files you have not picked.
  • Exported videos. Your exports are saved as MP4 files in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Saved projects. If you save a project, the app stores the location of the working copy, your trim start and end points, and whether vertical 9:16 format is on. This is kept on your phone only.

None of this leaves your device through the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not upload your videos to any server. We have no servers for this app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the app does not use an advertising ID.
  • We do not show ads, and there are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we never receive it.
  • There is no account or sign-in.

Sharing your videos

The app never shares anything by itself. If you tap "Share / Save exported video", Android opens its share menu and sends the file to the app you choose. That app handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

ClipForge AI does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location. Videos are opened only through the Android file picker, one file at a time, when you choose them.

Deleting your data

Working copies, exports and saved projects stay on your phone until you remove them. You can delete all of them at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. Copies you have already shared or saved to other apps or folders are not affected.

Children

ClipForge AI is a general video tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it collects no information at all.

Google Play

Google Play may collect its own information about installs and app performance under Google's privacy policy. We only see the general statistics Google provides to all developers.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
